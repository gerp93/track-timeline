How to Play

The goal

Build a timeline of songs in the order they were released. The first player to collect the target number of cards wins.

On your turn

A song plays for everyone at once. You decide where it belongs in your own timeline — before your earliest card, after your latest, or between any two of them — and lock in that position. Nobody sees the year until the round resolves.

Tokens

While a song is playing, anyone can name its artist and title. Get it right and you earn a token. Tokens are the only currency in the game, and they are worth more than they look.

Challenging

Once the current player locks in their placement, everyone else gets a chance to disagree. Spend a token and place the same song somewhere in your timeline. If the current player was wrong and you were right, you take the card instead of them.

The reveal

The release year comes out. The current player keeps the card if they placed it correctly. If they missed, the first challenger who got it right takes it. If nobody was right, the song is discarded and play moves on.

A note on secrecy

The song has to reach your browser in order to play, so a determined player with developer tools open can find out what it is. This game does not try to stop that — like the tabletop games it is based on, it assumes everyone would rather play than cheat.
