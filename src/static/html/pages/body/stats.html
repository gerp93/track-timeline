How to read these numbers
  • Placement accuracy counts a player's own-turn placements only. A steal is only possible after someone else missed, so steals are a harder, self-selected group and are reported on their own.
  • Every percentage shows how many tries it rests on. Bars with a dashed or faded look rest on fewer than 10 tries — treat them as hints. Hover any bar for the range the true figure plausibly lies in (95%).
  • Games are finished games with at least one opponent, rebuilt from the win log. Games nobody finished leave no record, and solo games count toward accuracy but not toward wins.
  • Named means the guess got the title (or the artist) right, as judged when the round ended.
  • Players who joined as room guests, and accounts that have since been deleted, are counted in table-wide figures but have no page of their own.
